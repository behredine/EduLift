Why interactive 3D simulators?

Textbooks describe anatomy and engineering in flat diagrams. EduLift builds them as 3D models you can rotate, explode into parts and explore at your own pace — turning abstract concepts into something you can see and touch.

  • Heart Lab — a 3D circulatory system simulator showing the chambers, valves and blood flow of the human heart.
  • Transformer Lab — a 3D power transformer simulator showing the core, coils and internals of a real transformer.
